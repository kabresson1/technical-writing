# Book Review - _Why Machines Learn: The Elegant Math Behind Modern AI_

## About This Article

This article is a review of the book _Why Machines Learn: The Elegant Math Behind Modern AI_ by [Anil Ananthaswamy](https://anilananthaswamy.com/). It summarizes the key insights and personal discoveries that I gathered while reading the book, with the goal of sharing my learnings with others who might be interested in diving deeper into the concepts behind AI and machine learning, especially from a mathematical standpoint.

The book review is structured into sections and sub-sections as follows:

* Introduction
* Key Insights
  * The History of AI Research and Influence from Other Fields
    * The MCP Neuron and the Perceptron
    * The Origins of Artificial Neural Networks
    * Inspirations from Physics
  * The Diversity of Applied Mathematics in Machine Learning and AI
    * How Probability and Statistics Shape AI Decision-Making
    * Principal Component Analysis: Where Statistics and Linear Algebra Intersect
    * Backpropagation: Algorithmic, Scalable Optimization
* Personal Learnings and Connections to Future Study
  * Key Takeaways
  * Looking Ahead

The article includes photographs that I took of the physical book, which are included for display purposes only. It also includes images generated with the assistance of GenAI to visualize my personal learnings from the book; these images do not reproduce verbatim excerpts from the book.
